# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in **Pixelman Adventure** a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone.  
We are committed to a harassment-free environment for contributors of all backgrounds and identities.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences  
- Providing constructive feedback and support  
- Showing empathy and kindness to others  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks  
- Public or private harassment  
- Publishing private information without permission  
- Discriminatory comments or jokes  

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by:
- Opening a **“Conduct”** issue on GitHub, or  
- Emailing the maintainer directly at **your_email@example.com**

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy of any individual reporting an incident.
